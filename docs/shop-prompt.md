Add a "Shop" and a "Dress up" screen to Dragon Brush (buttons on the kid's home). Kids spend the points they earn by brushing. Each kid has their own points, owned items and outfit. Item names in English and Hebrew.

SHOP
- Tabs per category: Hats, Fire, Glasses, Necklaces, Food, Dragons, Backgrounds, Shields.
- Item cards: a live 3D preview of the item on the kid's current dragon, name, price (⭐). Tap "Buy" → "Buy {name} for {price} points?" → "It's yours!" with a happy sound.
- Too expensive: "Keep brushing to get {n} more points!". Owned items show "Owned".
- Gift items are never sold; their card is locked and says how to get them.
- "Special" tab only around a holiday: "{holiday} treats, only for a short time! Once you buy one, it's yours forever." Cards tagged "Limited".

DRESS UP
- Slots: Dragon, Hat, Glasses, Necklace, Fire, Food, Background. Only owned items; tap to wear, with a big live 3D preview and a "Wearing" tag. Every accessory fits every dragon.
- Fire = the fire color in the game. Food = what appears on the corner targets (burns, then gets eaten; broccoli makes the dragon go "yuck!" but still counts). Background = a scene behind the dragon instead of the camera picture; the camera shows in a small window.
- On a holiday or birthday week, a costume fills the slots the kid left empty, just for that day.
